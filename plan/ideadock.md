2. Power Systems:
Fusion Reactor: Powers all systems and generates energy for wormhole creation.
Solar Panels: Efficient in well-lit areas but weak in deep space.
RTG (Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator): Reliable long-term power, but weak output.
Nuclear Reactor: High power but requires cooling and shielding.
Battery Banks: Stores excess energy; needed for power surges.
Supercapacitors: Fast energy storage for burst power needs.
3. Propulsion Systems:
Reaction Control Thrusters (RCS): Small maneuvering jets for precise movement.
Ion Thrusters: Highly efficient but slow acceleration.
Hall-effect Thrusters: More powerful than standard ion thrusters.
Chemical Rockets: High thrust but consumes propellant quickly.
Plasma Thrusters: More efficient than chemical but require a lot of power.
Solar Sails: No fuel needed but slow acceleration, useful for long-term travel.
4. Jump Systems:
Jump Coils: Advanced navigation systems that track spacetime resonance and gamma-ray bursts generated during wormhole creation.
Wormhole Sensors: Monitors the status of the wormhole—its stability, resonance, and gravitational anomalies nearby.
Wormhole Stabilizers: Ensures the wormhole remains stable during creation and entry.
Inertial Dampeners: Protects the crew and ship from harmful acceleration or deceleration.
Time Compensation Systems: Helps manage time distortions during wormhole travel.
Exit Stabilizers: Ensures smooth transition from the wormhole to normal space.
5. Communication Systems:
High-Gain Antenna: Directs signals over long distances with high power.
Low-Gain Antenna: Omnidirectional but short-range.
Laser Communications: High bandwidth, but requires precise targeting.
Quantum Entanglement Comms (Experimental): Instant communication, requires exotic materials.
Mesh Network Relays: Automated drones that act as signal boosters.
6. Sensors & Navigation:
LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging): For mapping and detecting obstacles.
RADAR (Radio Detection and Ranging): Works in various environments, including atmospheres.
Gravimetric Sensors: Detects mass concentrations (useful for hidden objects).
Star Tracker: Uses star positions for precise navigation.
Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU): Tracks movement when other navigation is unavailable.
7. Resource Collection & Processing:
Mining Laser: Cuts into asteroids for raw materials.
Drill System: More efficient for extracting deep materials.
Electromagnetic Scoop: Captures charged particles in space (fuel collection).
Refinery Module: Converts raw ore into usable materials.
3D Printer & Fabricator: Constructs new components from refined resources.
8. Construction & Automation:
Docking Ports (Multiple Sizes): Connects ships and stations.
Robotic Assembly Arms: For automated construction and repair.
Automated Drone Bays: Deploys small repair or mining drones.
Orbital Shipyard Core: A large facility that builds and maintains spacecraft.
Factory Module: Automates production of components.
Storage Containers:
Gas Tanks: Stores fuel or breathable atmosphere.
Ore Containers: Holds raw mined materials.
Manufactured Goods Containers: Stores refined parts.
9. Defense & Durability:
Whipple Shielding: Protects against micrometeorites.
Heat Radiators: Dissipates heat from reactors and engines.
EMP Shielding: Protects against electronic warfare.
Armor Plating:
Titanium Alloy: Lightweight and strong.
Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymers: Heat-resistant and lightweight.
Whipple Multi-Layered Armor: Best for resisting high-velocity impacts.
10. Station & Dockyard Modules:
Command Hub: The main AI control center for a station.
Fuel Depot: Stores and distributes fuel for visiting ships.
Gravity Ring: Provides artificial gravity for long-term habitation.
Solar Farm: Large-scale energy production for stations.
Magnetic Docking Rails: Helps guide and secure incoming ships.

Raw Resources:
Metallic Elements: Iron, Nickel, Titanium, Tungsten, Aluminum.
Exotic & Rare Elements: Lithium, Platinum, Iridium, Uranium, Thorium.
Non-Metallic Elements: Silicon, Carbon, Helium-3.
Refined & Synthetic Materials:
Basic Alloys: Steel, Titanium Alloy, Superalloy.
Advanced Alloys: Refractory Alloy, Memory Metal, Radiation-Resistant Alloy.
Specialized Materials: Aerogel, Carbon Nanotubes, Graphene Sheets.
Crystal Growth & High-Tech Materials:
Optical & Laser Crystals: Sapphire, YAG, Quartz.
Energy & Computing Crystals: Gallium Arsenide, Perovskite, Quantum Dots.
Exotic & Experimental Crystals: Metamaterials, Superconductive Crystals.
High-Energy Physics & Particle Accelerators:
Functions: Antimatter Production, Strange Matter Synthesis, Muon Catalysis, Dark Matter Research.

Ore Refinery: Extracts pure elements.

High-Pressure Forge: Manufactures high-stress components.
