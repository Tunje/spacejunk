# Solar panel
Priority 14 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_solar_panel_mk1.png`. Model: `solar_panel_mk1.glb`

A solar panel that bolts flat onto the side of a spacecraft: a thick, solid rectangular slab, 3.0 m by 2.0 m and 0.3 m thick, with rounded corners. A thick mid-grey frame about 0.2 m wide runs round the edge, and the back is mid grey. The front face carries six big square cell tiles in satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal, in 2 rows of 3, separated by wide mid-grey grooves about 0.15 m across. No fine cell lines. Clean and simple.

Real size: 3.0 m wide (X), 2.0 m tall (Y), 0.3 m deep (Z) from the mounting face. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the mounting plate's flat back face. That face points along -Z and the part extends from Z = 0 to Z = +0.3 m. Up is +Y.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: gold #c9a227 for the cell tiles, mid grey #8a8d91 for the frame, grooves and back.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from in front and slightly to the side, so the tiled face and its thick edge both show.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
