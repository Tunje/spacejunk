# Goods storage
Priority 9 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_goods_storage_mk1.png`. Model: `goods_storage_mk1.glb`

A big storage module for finished goods that bolts onto a station: a large box, square in section and a little longer than it is wide, with crisply bevelled edges. At the back, a thick flat square mid-grey mounting plate the full 3.5 m wide and tall. The body is mid grey, built from bolted plates: crisp panel-seam grooves divide each side into a 2 by 2 grid. Two wide off-white bands, each about 0.6 m wide, wrap round the body from top to sides to bottom, flush with the surface and edged by crisp grooves. On the front end face, a large recessed square mid-grey panel about 2.4 m across. Bulky, orderly and clean, clearly a cargo module.

Real size: 3.5 m wide (X), 3.5 m tall (Y), 4.0 m deep (Z) from the mounting face. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the mounting plate's flat back face. That face points along -Z and the part extends from Z = 0 to Z = +4.0 m. Up is +Y.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: mid grey #8a8d91 for the body and mounting plate, off-white #e8e4d8 for the two wide bands.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above and in front, so the front end, one side and the top all show, with the mounting plate's edge visible at the back.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Off-white on white: keep the off-white areas clearly darker than the pure white background everywhere, with a soft mid-tone shade towards their edges, so the outline stays easy to separate. No outline stroke.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
