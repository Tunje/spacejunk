# Mining laser
Priority 1 (Newman Probe style test). Image: `newmanprobe_mining_laser_mk1.png`. Model: `mining_laser_mk1.glb`

A stubby industrial mining laser that bolts onto the side of a spacecraft: a chunky safety-orange box-shaped body with well-rounded corners, slightly longer than it is wide. At the back is a thick flat square mid-grey mounting plate with rounded corners, a little wider than the body, which is the face that bolts to the ship. At the front, the body steps down through one thick mid-grey round collar into a short, fat cylindrical emitter barrel, also mid grey, ending in a wide flat round lens face in foil gold. Three thick, widely spaced ribs wrap across the top of the body as solid raised bands, in orange, suggesting cooling. It looks heavy, simple and clean, like a compact piece of mining equipment, slightly oversized and a little toy-like in its proportions.

Real size: 1.4 m long from mounting plate to lens, 0.8 m wide, 0.8 m tall. Length sets the model's scale.
Materials: satin painted metal for the body and collars, a smooth solid gold-coloured surface for the lens, not glass. Colours: safety orange #e8641b for the body, mid grey #8a8d91 for the plate, collar and barrel, foil gold #c9a227 for the lens.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, with the lens pointing forward and to the right, so the lens face, one side and the top all show, and the mounting plate's edge is visible at the back.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed mesh with no moving parts. The barrel is fixed.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No beam, glow or sparks. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No grilles, fine vents, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
