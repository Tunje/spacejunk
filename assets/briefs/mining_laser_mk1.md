# Mining laser
Priority 17 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_mining_laser_mk1.png`. Model: `mining_laser_mk1.glb`

A stubby industrial mining laser that bolts onto the side of a spacecraft: a chunky safety-orange box-shaped body with well-rounded corners, 0.8 m wide and 0.8 m tall, slightly longer than it is wide. At the back, a thick flat square mid-grey mounting plate with rounded corners, the full 0.8 m wide and tall, which is the face that bolts to the ship. At the front, the body steps down through one thick mid-grey round collar into a short, fat cylindrical emitter barrel, also mid grey, ending in a wide flat round lens face in satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal, not glass. Three thick, widely spaced orange ribs wrap across the top and sides of the body as raised bands, suggesting cooling. Heavy, simple and clean. No beam, glow or sparks.

Real size: 0.8 m wide (X), 0.8 m tall (Y), 1.4 m long (Z) from the mounting face to the lens. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the mounting plate's flat back face. That face points along -Z and the part extends from Z = 0 to Z = +1.4 m. Up is +Y.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: safety orange #e8641b for the body and ribs, mid grey #8a8d91 for the plate, collar and barrel, gold #c9a227 for the lens.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, with the lens pointing forward and to the right, so the lens face, one side and the top all show, and the mounting plate's edge is visible at the back.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
