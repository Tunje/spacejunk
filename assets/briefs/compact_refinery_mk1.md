# Compact refinery
Priority 20 (Newman Probe batch B22). Image: `newmanprobe_compact_refinery_mk1.png`. Model: `compact_refinery_mk1.glb`

A small ore refinery that bolts onto the side of a spacecraft, a compact cousin of the big station refinery. At the back, a thick flat mid-grey mounting plate the full 1.4 m wide and 1.6 m tall, whose centre is the middle of the part. In front of it, on its lower part, a safety-orange box 1.3 m tall with crisply bevelled edges, so every face is flat with a narrow angled facet between. Standing on top of the box, towards the front, one squat mid-grey eight-sided furnace tower 0.6 m across and 0.3 m tall, with a flat top. On the front face, a recessed square mid-grey ore intake 0.6 m across. Crisp panel-seam grooves divide each side face in two. Compact, industrial and clean.
