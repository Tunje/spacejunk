# Battery bank
Priority 7 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_battery_bank_mk1.png`. Model: `battery_bank_mk1.glb`

A battery that bolts onto the side of a spacecraft. At the back, a thick flat square mid-grey mounting plate with rounded corners, the full 1.2 m wide and tall. In front of it, a chunky mid-grey case with well-rounded corners. Its front face carries four big off-white rounded square cell blocks in a 2 by 2 grid, each standing out about 0.1 m, separated by wide grooves about 0.1 m across. Compact, heavy and clean.

Real size: 1.2 m wide (X), 1.2 m tall (Y), 0.8 m deep (Z) from the mounting face. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the mounting plate's flat back face. That face points along -Z and the part extends from Z = 0 to Z = +0.8 m. Up is +Y.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: mid grey #8a8d91 for the case and mounting plate, off-white #e8e4d8 for the four cell blocks.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
Connectors (from data/modules.json, metres from the origin): mount at (0, 0, 0) facing -Z. Each is a flat spot square to its facing direction, flush with the surface there. If generation bends one, flatten it afterwards; core spots sit in the recessed core bay.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above and in front, so the front face, one side and the top all show, with the mounting plate's edge visible at the back.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Off-white on white: keep the off-white areas clearly darker than the pure white background everywhere, with a soft mid-tone shade towards their edges, so the outline stays easy to separate. No outline stroke.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
