# Small hull
Priority 2 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_small_hull.png`. Model: `small_hull.glb`

The hull of a small crewless drone: a short, stubby upright cylinder, a little taller than it is wide. A smooth off-white painted body exactly 1.0 m across. Thick mid-grey collars about 0.15 m tall at the top and bottom, exactly as wide as the body, with flat end faces and slightly rounded outer edges. One mid-grey band about 0.12 m tall runs round the body flush with its surface, centred 0.85 m up. Parts bolt on in two rings at 0.5 and 1.2 m up, so the body at those heights must be plain, smooth off-white. Compact, sturdy and clean, a little toy-like. It stands alone, with nothing attached.

Real size: 1.6 m tall (Y), 1.0 m in diameter (X and Z). 1.0 m is the widest point, collars included. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the flat bottom face, which points along -Y. The top face is at Y = +1.6 m.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: off-white #e8e4d8 for the body, mid grey #8a8d91 for the end collars and band.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, standing upright, so the top end face and the curve of the body both show.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Off-white on white: keep the off-white areas clearly darker than the pure white background everywhere, with a soft mid-tone shade towards their edges, so the outline stays easy to separate. No outline stroke.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
