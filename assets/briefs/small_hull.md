# Small hull
Priority 2 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_small_hull.png`. Model: `small_hull.glb`

The hull of a small crewless drone: a short, stubby upright eight-sided prism, a little taller than it is wide, not a round drum. The body is 1.0 m across between opposite flat faces, which point along +X, -X, +Z, -Z and the four diagonals. The top 0.2 m and bottom 0.2 m are mid-grey shoulders tapering inwards to a flat round mounting face about 0.6 m across, with a thick rim. Crisp panel-seam grooves run down every corner and across every face at 0.85 m up. On the +X face only, centred 1.2 m up, a recessed square mid-grey core bay about 0.3 m across. Parts bolt onto the centres of the +X, -X, +Z and -Z faces at 0.5 m and 1.2 m up, so those spots are plain off-white plate. Compact, sturdy and clean, clearly a little spacecraft, never a can. It stands alone, with nothing attached.

Real size: 1.6 m tall (Y), 1.0 m across the flats (X and Z). Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the flat bottom face, which points along -Y. The top face is at Y = +1.6 m.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: off-white #e8e4d8 for the body plating, mid grey #8a8d91 for the shoulders, mounting faces and core bay.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, standing upright, looking at a corner between two flat faces, so the top mounting face, two body faces and the core bay all show.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Off-white on white: keep the off-white areas clearly darker than the pure white background everywhere, with a soft mid-tone shade towards their edges, so the outline stays easy to separate. No outline stroke.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
