# Command hub
Priority 3 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_command_hub.png`. Model: `command_hub.glb`

The core drum of a crewless space station: a big, wide upright cylinder, a little wider than it is tall. A smooth off-white painted body exactly 5.0 m across, with straight sides. Thick mid-grey collars about 0.4 m tall at the top and bottom, exactly as wide as the body, with flat end faces and slightly rounded outer edges. Halfway up, on two opposite sides, sits a round mid-grey port pad about 1.5 m across, set flush into the body like a flat round lid, ringed by a crisp groove. Modules bolt on all round the body between the collars, so apart from the two pads the body is plain, smooth off-white. Solid, calm and clean, like the hub everything else is built round. It stands alone, with nothing attached.

Real size: 6.0 m tall (Y), 5.0 m in diameter (X and Z). 5.0 m is the widest point, collars and pads included. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the flat bottom face, which points along -Y. The top face is at Y = +6.0 m.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: off-white #e8e4d8 for the body, mid grey #8a8d91 for the end collars and the two port pads.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, standing upright, so the top end face, the curve of the body and one port pad all show.
Triangle target: 12000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Off-white on white: keep the off-white areas clearly darker than the pure white background everywhere, with a soft mid-tone shade towards their edges, so the outline stays easy to separate. No outline stroke.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
