# Ore storage
Priority 10 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_ore_storage_mk1.png`. Model: `ore_storage_mk1.glb`

A big storage module for raw ore that bolts onto a station: a fat cylinder 3.5 m across lying on its side, its axis running straight out from the mounting plate. At the back, a thick flat square mid-grey mounting plate the full 3.5 m wide and tall. The drum is mid grey, with thick rounded mid-grey end caps. Two thick safety-orange hoop bands, each about 0.35 m wide, wrap round the drum, standing out slightly, at one third and two thirds of its length. Heavy, rugged and clean, clearly a drum rather than a box.

Real size: 3.5 m wide (X), 3.5 m tall (Y), 4.0 m deep (Z) from the mounting face. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the mounting plate's flat back face. That face points along -Z and the part extends from Z = 0 to Z = +4.0 m. Up is +Y.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: mid grey #8a8d91 for the drum, end caps and mounting plate, safety orange #e8641b for the two hoop bands.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above and in front, so the front end cap, the curve of the drum and the top all show, with the mounting plate's edge visible at the back.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
