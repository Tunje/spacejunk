# Compact fabricator
Priority 21 (Newman Probe batch B22). Image: `newmanprobe_compact_fabricator_mk1.png`. Model: `compact_fabricator_mk1.glb`

A small factory module that bolts onto the side of a spacecraft, a compact cousin of the station fabricator. At the back, a thick flat square mid-grey mounting plate the full 1.4 m wide and tall. In front of it, a safety-orange cube with crisply bevelled edges. The front face carries a shallow mid-grey eight-sided dome 0.9 m across, faceted rather than round, like the housing of a work head. The four vertical edges carry thick flat mid-grey bumper strips 0.15 m wide from top to bottom. Busy-looking but simple, and clean.
