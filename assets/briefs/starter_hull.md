# Starter hull
Priority 1 (Newman Probe style test). Image: `newmanprobe_starter_hull.png`. Model: `starter_hull.glb`

The central spine of a small crewless spacecraft: one short, fat upright cylinder that other parts bolt onto. It has a smooth off-white painted body with softly rounded shoulders at both ends. A thick mid-grey flanged collar caps the top and another caps the bottom, each a chunky round disc, slightly wider than the body, with a flat end face. Three broad mid-grey bands wrap around the body, at about one sixth, two fifths and seven eighths of the way up, each a raised solid ring about 0.3 m tall. Between the second and third bands sits one wide wrap of smooth foil gold insulation, about 1.2 m tall, running all the way round. It looks sturdy, simple and clean, like a well-made pressure vessel, slightly oversized and a little toy-like in its proportions. It stands alone, with nothing attached.

Real size: 6.0 m tall, 2.5 m in diameter. Height sets the model's scale.
Materials: satin painted metal for the body and bands, smooth foil for the gold wrap. Colours: off-white #e8e4d8 for the body, mid grey #8a8d91 for the collars and bands, foil gold #c9a227 for the wrap.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, standing upright, so the top end face and the curve of the body both show.
Triangle target: 12000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed mesh with no moving parts.

Off-white on white (this is what the test checks): keep the body clearly darker than the pure white background everywhere. Let the even light give it a soft mid-tone shade towards its edges, so its whole outline stays easy to separate from the background. The body must never fade into the background, and it needs no outline stroke.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
