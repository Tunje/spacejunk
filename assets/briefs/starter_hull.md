# Starter hull
Priority 1 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_starter_hull.png`. Model: `starter_hull.glb`

The central spine of a small crewless spacecraft: one short, fat upright cylinder that other parts bolt onto. A smooth off-white painted body, a straight-sided cylinder exactly 2.5 m across. The top and bottom ends are thick mid-grey collars, each about 0.3 m tall, exactly as wide as the body, with flat end faces and slightly rounded outer edges. Two mid-grey bands, each about 0.3 m tall, run round the body flush with its surface, painted on rather than raised, with a crisp shallow groove along each edge: one centred 1.7 m up from the base and one centred 4.5 m up. One wide wrap of satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal insulation, also flush, runs all the way round from 2.7 m to 3.5 m up. Parts bolt on in four rings at 1.0, 2.4, 3.8 and 5.2 m up, so the body at those heights must be plain, smooth off-white with nothing on it. Sturdy, simple and clean, like a well-made pressure vessel, slightly toy-like in its proportions. It stands alone, with nothing attached.

Real size: 6.0 m tall (Y), 2.5 m in diameter (X and Z). 2.5 m is the widest point, collars included. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the flat bottom face, which points along -Y. The top face is at Y = +6.0 m.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: off-white #e8e4d8 for the body, mid grey #8a8d91 for the end collars and bands, gold #c9a227 for the wrap.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, standing upright, so the top end face and the curve of the body both show.
Triangle target: 12000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Off-white on white: keep the off-white areas clearly darker than the pure white background everywhere, with a soft mid-tone shade towards their edges, so the outline stays easy to separate. No outline stroke.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
