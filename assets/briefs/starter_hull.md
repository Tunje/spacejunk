# Starter hull
Priority 1 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_starter_hull.png`. Model: `starter_hull.glb`

SHAPE, read this first: an EIGHT-SIDED PRISM: eight flat rectangular faces and eight sharp straight vertical corners, like an octagonal column. It is NOT a cylinder: no round body, no curved sides, no round lids. Only the gold panels and the core bay break the flat faces.

The central spine of a small crewless spacecraft that other parts bolt onto: an upright eight-sided prism, not a round drum. The body is 2.5 m across between opposite flat faces, and its eight flat faces point straight along +X, -X, +Z, -Z and the four diagonals between. The top 0.5 m and bottom 0.5 m are mid-grey shoulders that taper inwards, like a cut-off pyramid, to a flat eight-sided mounting face about 1.5 m across at each end, with a thick rim. The off-white body between the shoulders reads as bolted plating: crisp narrow panel-seam grooves run down every corner, and across every face at 1.7 m, 3.1 m and 4.5 m up. On the four diagonal faces, between 2.6 m and 3.6 m up, sit recessed rectangular insulation panels in satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. On the +X face only, centred 3.8 m up, a recessed square mid-grey core bay about 0.8 m across, so the hull looks different on one side. Parts bolt onto the centres of the +X, -X, +Z and -Z faces at 1.0, 2.4 and 5.2 m up, so those spots are plain off-white plate. Sturdy, purposeful and clean, clearly the hull of a small spacecraft, never a barrel or tin. It stands alone, with nothing attached.

Real size: 6.0 m tall (Y), 2.5 m across the flats (X and Z). Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the flat bottom face, which points along -Y. The top face is at Y = +6.0 m.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: off-white #e8e4d8 for the body plating, mid grey #8a8d91 for the shoulders, mounting faces and core bay, gold #c9a227 for the insulation panels.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
