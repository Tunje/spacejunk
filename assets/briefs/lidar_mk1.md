# LIDAR
Priority 16 (Newman Probe batch B12). Image: `newmanprobe_lidar_mk1.png`. Model: `lidar_mk1.glb`

A scanner that bolts onto the side of a spacecraft: a small, chunky safety-orange box with well-rounded corners, square in section. At the back, a thick flat square mid-grey mounting plate the full 0.6 m wide and tall. On the front face, a fat round mid-grey dome housing about 0.45 m across, with a wide flat round lens face in satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal at its front. Small, neat and clean.

Real size: 0.6 m wide (X), 0.6 m tall (Y), 0.8 m deep (Z) from the mounting face. Scale by the longest side.
Placement (set after generation): origin at the centre of the mounting plate's flat back face. That face points along -Z and the part extends from Z = 0 to Z = +0.8 m. Up is +Y.
Materials: satin painted metal; anything gold is satin gold, non-mirror: a soft painted sheen, never a polished reflective metal. Colours: safety orange #e8641b for the body, mid grey #8a8d91 for the mounting plate and dome housing, gold #c9a227 for the lens.
Every collar, band, rib, rim and groove has crisp machined edges: true circles and straight lines, evenly thick all the way round, with no hand-moulded lumps, dents or wobble.
View: 3/4 view from slightly above, with the dome pointing forward and to the right, so the lens, one side and the top all show, with the mounting plate's edge visible at the back.
Triangle target: 6000. Set this before generation; it cannot be reduced afterwards. One solid closed shape with no moving parts.

Picture rules (for the 3D step): whole object in frame, 3/4 view, pure white background, nothing else in the picture, no floor, no floor shadow, even soft lighting. Solid readable mid-tone colours, not near-black. At least 1024 px. No thin parts: anything that would be thin must be drawn thick and solid. No lattices, grilles, rails, pipes, cables or antennas. No windows, hatches, ladders or people. No stripes, decals, logos, lettering or numbers. No rust, dirt or damage. Original design: not based on any existing game, film or real spacecraft.
